Incident Severity Classification & On-Call Response Matrix

Schedule F operational incident response timelines, triage criteria, and escalation triggers.

P1

P1 - Critical Outage

MTTA (Acknowledge): ≤ 15 Minutes (24/7/365)
MTTR (Resolution): ≤ 60 Minutes
Cadence: Every 15 mins in War Room

Triggers: Complete checkout freeze, catalog inaccessible, database failover hang, verified ransomware or data breach.

Instant War Room & Incident Commander
P2

P2 - Major Degradation

MTTA: ≤ 30 Minutes (24/7/365)
MTTR: ≤ 4 Hours
Cadence: Every 60 minutes

Triggers: Single payment gateway offline (fallback working), search degraded, SAP ERP sync queue backlog > 30 mins.

Senior Architect Escalation
P3

P3 - Minor Impairment

MTTA: ≤ 2 Hours (Business Hours)
MTTR: ≤ 24 Hours
Cadence: Daily Ticket Update

Triggers: Admin CMS reporting timeout, coupon banner cosmetic alignment defect, non-blocking telemetry delay.

Sprint Backlog Queue
P4

P4 - Informational

MTTA: ≤ 8 Hours (Business Hours)
MTTR: ≤ 5 Business Days
Cadence: Upon milestone change

Triggers: Documentation inquiry, cosmetic styling suggestion, minor administrative inquiry.

Standard Triage

Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Protocol

For all P1 incidents and major P2 incidents, Provider must deliver a blameless 5-Whys RCA document within 5 business days, including chronological timeline, root cause, and Corrective and Preventative Actions (CAPA).
