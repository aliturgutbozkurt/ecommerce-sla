# Schedule E: Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**Document Reference:** NDA-2026-OMNISTORE-001  
**Associated MSA:** MSA-2026-APEX-CS-001  
**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  

---

## 1. PURPOSE & INTENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") is entered into by and between **ApexRetail Global A.Ş. / Inc.** ("ApexRetail") and **CloudScale Systems Yazılım A.Ş.** ("CloudScale") to protect valuable proprietary information, source code, system architectures, algorithmic models, and commercial secrets exchanged during the development and management of the **OmniStore SaaS & Unified Commerce Engine**.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other Party ("Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, electronically, or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure, including without limitation:

1.  **Technical Data:** Software source code, compiled binaries, database schemas, system architecture diagrams, REST/GraphQL API specifications, cryptographic keys, penetration testing reports, and disaster recovery blueprints.
2.  **Commercial & Strategic Information:** Customer transaction volumes, pricing formulas, discount tiers, vendor agreements, supplier lists, marketing campaign calendars, and strategic product roadmaps.
3.  **Customer Data:** All end-consumer records, transaction logs, shopper behavior metrics, and order histories belonging to ApexRetail.

---

## 3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY

Confidential Information shall not include information that:
1.  Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
2.  Was already lawfully in the rightful possession of the Receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure;
3.  Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;
4.  Is rightfully received from an independent third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

---

## 4. OBLIGATIONS & STANDARD OF CARE

The Receiving Party agrees to:
*   Protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like kind, but in no event less than a **reasonable and robust standard of enterprise care**.
*   Restrict access to Confidential Information strictly to employees, contractors, legal counsel, and financial auditors who have a bona fide "need-to-know" for the performance of the MSA and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this NDA.
*   Not copy, reverse engineer, reproduce, or distribute Confidential Information except as strictly required to fulfill the objectives of the MSA.

---

## 5. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

If the Receiving Party is compelled by valid court order, subpoena, or statutory regulatory agency to disclose Confidential Information, it shall:
1.  Provide the Disclosing Party with prompt written notice (to the extent legally permissible) to enable the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order;
2.  Disclose only that portion of Confidential Information strictly mandated by law.

---

## 6. TERM OF CONFIDENTIALITY

The obligations under this NDA shall remain in full force during the Term of the MSA and shall survive for a period of **five (5) years** following expiration or termination; provided, however, that trade secrets, proprietary algorithms, and source code shall remain confidential in **perpetuity**.

---

## 7. INJUNCTIVE RELIEF

The Parties acknowledge that any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information will cause irreparable injury for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief, specific performance, and equitable remedies from any court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of posting a bond.
